# SEO Optimization Guide

Guidelines for optimizing Enterpret content for search visibility and organic traffic.

---

## SEO Content Workflow

### Before Writing

1. **Keyword Research**
   - Identify primary keyword (main search term)
   - Identify 3-5 secondary keywords (related terms)
   - Check search volume and competition
   - Analyze top-ranking content for the keyword

2. **Search Intent Analysis**
   - **Informational**: "what is voice of customer" → educational content
   - **Navigational**: "enterpret login" → product pages
   - **Commercial**: "best feedback analytics tools" → comparison/listicle
   - **Transactional**: "enterpret pricing" → conversion pages

3. **Content Gap Analysis**
   - What do top results cover?
   - What's missing that we can add?
   - What unique angle can Enterpret provide?

## On-Page SEO Checklist

### Title Tag (55-60 characters)
- [ ] Primary keyword near the beginning
- [ ] Compelling and clickable
- [ ] Unique across the site
- [ ] Includes brand name (optional, at end)

**Formula**: [Primary Keyword]: [Benefit/Hook] | Enterpret

**Examples**:
- "Voice of Customer Programs: The Complete Guide | Enterpret"
- "Customer Feedback Analysis: 5 Methods That Actually Work"
- "How to Build a Customer Intelligence Strategy in 2026"

### Meta Description (150-160 characters)
- [ ] Includes primary keyword naturally
- [ ] Summarizes content value
- [ ] Includes call-to-action
- [ ] Unique and compelling

**Formula**: [What the content covers] + [Why it matters/benefit] + [CTA hint]

**Example**:
"Learn how leading companies analyze customer feedback at scale. Discover the frameworks, tools, and strategies that turn feedback chaos into actionable intelligence."

### URL Structure
- [ ] Short and descriptive
- [ ] Includes primary keyword
- [ ] Uses hyphens, not underscores
- [ ] No unnecessary words (the, and, of)

**Good**: `/blog/voice-of-customer-program-guide`
**Bad**: `/blog/the-ultimate-guide-to-building-a-voice-of-customer-program-for-your-company`

### Headings (H1, H2, H3)
- [ ] One H1 per page (usually the title)
- [ ] H2s for main sections (include secondary keywords)
- [ ] H3s for subsections
- [ ] Logical hierarchy (don't skip levels)
- [ ] Scannable and descriptive

### Content Body
- [ ] Primary keyword in first 100 words
- [ ] Secondary keywords distributed naturally
- [ ] Keyword density 1-2% (don't stuff)
- [ ] Internal links to related Enterpret content
- [ ] External links to authoritative sources
- [ ] Images with descriptive alt text

## Keyword Strategy by Content Type

### Blog Posts (Thought Leadership)
**Target**: Informational keywords, long-tail questions

**Examples**:
- "what is customer intelligence"
- "how to analyze customer feedback"
- "voice of customer best practices"
- "customer feedback vs customer data"

**Structure**: Answer the question early, then go deep

### Blog Posts (Product-Led)
**Target**: Commercial keywords, feature-specific terms

**Examples**:
- "feedback analytics software"
- "customer feedback categorization tool"
- "ai feedback analysis"
- "enterpret vs [competitor]"

**Structure**: Problem → Solution → How Enterpret helps → CTA

### Case Studies
**Target**: Brand + use case keywords

**Examples**:
- "[Customer] customer feedback case study"
- "how [industry] companies use feedback analytics"
- "[outcome] with customer intelligence"

**Structure**: Optimize for the outcome/use case, not just the customer name

### Guides & Pillar Content
**Target**: High-volume, competitive head terms

**Examples**:
- "voice of customer"
- "customer feedback analysis"
- "customer intelligence platform"

**Structure**: Comprehensive coverage, internal links to supporting content

## Content Structure for SEO

### The Skyscraper Approach
1. Find content ranking for your target keyword
2. Analyze what makes it rank (depth, freshness, format)
3. Create something significantly better
4. Promote to earn backlinks

### Topic Clusters
**Pillar page**: Comprehensive guide on broad topic
- Example: "The Complete Guide to Voice of Customer"

**Cluster content**: Supporting posts on specific subtopics
- "How to Build a VoC Program"
- "VoC Metrics That Matter"
- "VoC Tools Comparison"
- "VoC for Product Teams"

**Internal linking**: All cluster content links to pillar; pillar links to clusters

## Technical SEO Reminders

### Images
- Descriptive file names: `customer-feedback-dashboard.png` not `IMG_1234.png`
- Alt text with keywords: "Enterpret dashboard showing customer feedback trends"
- Compress for fast loading
- Use WebP format when possible

### Internal Links
- Link to relevant Enterpret blog posts, product pages, case studies
- Use descriptive anchor text (not "click here")
- Aim for 3-5 internal links per post
- Update old posts to link to new content

### External Links
- Link to authoritative sources (research, industry reports)
- Use for citations and credibility
- Open in new tab for user experience
- Nofollow for sponsored/untrusted links

## Content Refresh Strategy

### When to Update
- Rankings dropped for target keywords
- Information is outdated (stats, tools, best practices)
- New Enterpret features relevant to the topic
- Competitors published better content

### How to Refresh
1. Update outdated stats and examples
2. Add new sections for comprehensiveness
3. Improve internal linking
4. Refresh meta title/description
5. Update publication date
6. Re-promote on social

## SEO Metrics to Track

| Metric | What It Tells You |
|--------|-------------------|
| Organic traffic | Overall SEO performance |
| Keyword rankings | Visibility for target terms |
| Click-through rate (CTR) | Title/description effectiveness |
| Bounce rate | Content relevance to search intent |
| Time on page | Content engagement |
| Backlinks | Authority and promotion success |
| Conversions from organic | Business impact |

## Quick SEO Audit

Before publishing, verify:

- [ ] **Title**: Keyword + compelling, under 60 chars
- [ ] **Meta description**: Keyword + value prop, under 160 chars
- [ ] **URL**: Clean, keyword-included
- [ ] **H1**: Matches title, includes keyword
- [ ] **H2s**: Cover key subtopics, include secondary keywords
- [ ] **First paragraph**: Keyword appears naturally
- [ ] **Images**: Alt text, compressed, descriptive names
- [ ] **Internal links**: 3-5 to relevant Enterpret content
- [ ] **External links**: 1-3 to authoritative sources
- [ ] **Word count**: Appropriate for topic (usually 1,500+ for competitive terms)
- [ ] **Readability**: Short paragraphs, scannable headings
