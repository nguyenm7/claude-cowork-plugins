# Enterpret Brand & Voice Guide

**Archetype**: Sage-Architect (wise, systematic, transformative)

---

## Core Framework

### Hook + Anchor

**Hook (Problem → Promise)**:
> "Customer feedback is everywhere. Customer Intelligence is here."

**Anchor (Category Definition)**:
> Customer Intelligence unifies every customer signal, understands it in business context, and drives action across the org.

**Flow Rule**: Hook → Anchor → Narrative Spine → Pillars

Always pair the Hook with an **outcome-led subheader or proof badge**:
- "Insights in hours, not weeks"
- "10× more feedback processed with the same team"
- "40% fewer human inquiries"

---

## Narrative Spines (Emotional Arcs)

Use these to frame problems before introducing solutions:

| Spine | Pattern | Example |
|-------|---------|---------|
| Chaos → Clarity | Overwhelming → Organized | "Feedback is everywhere. Enterpret unifies it into actionable intelligence." |
| Generic → Specific | One-size-fits-all → Tailored | "Generic AI gives generic insights. Our AI learns your business language." |
| Reactive → Proactive | After-the-fact → Ahead of issues | "VoC reports what happened. Customer Intelligence drives action now." |

**Rule**: Lead with a spine (emotional hook), resolve with pillars (rational proof).

---

## Pillars (Logical Proof)

| Pillar | One-liner | What it means |
|--------|-----------|---------------|
| **Unify** | All feedback, one truth | Connect every signal across tools and teams |
| **Understand** | AI that learns your language | Context-aware analysis, not generic categorization |
| **Act** | Insights that drive action | Turn intelligence into owned work, not just reports |

## Tone Guidelines

### Blend
- **Conversational & Empathetic** + **Authoritative & Visionary**
- Start with empathy (pain in customer's words)
- Resolve with vision (how Enterpret transforms)

### Tone Ratios by Channel

| Channel | Approachable | Visionary |
|---------|--------------|-----------|
| Ads / Social | 70% | 30% |
| Product pages | 50% | 50% |
| Exec decks / Blogs | 40% | 60% |

### Writing Principles

1. **Lead with Empathy**: "Your team is drowning in feedback."
2. **Land with Vision**: "Enterpret adapts to your business and drives confident action."
3. **Create Urgency**: Stress compounding advantage. Delay = irrelevance.
4. **Keep it Human**: Short, direct sentences. Favor "you/your."
5. **Be Bold, Not Arrogant**: Confident statements, grounded in proof.

- spreadsheet hell

### Solution Language (Introduce after pain)
- AI categorization
- adaptive taxonomy
- knowledge graph
- anomaly detection
- agentic workflows
- owned work
- real-time intelligence

### Deprecated (Avoid)
- buckets
- repository
- chatbot
- omnichannel
- longitudinal
- generic AI
- empty hype: "game-changer," "revolutionary," "best-in-class"

## Channel-Specific Guidance

### Website Hero (First Touch)
- Use the Hook (primary)
- Pair with outcome-led subheader + proof badge
- Introduce U→U→A below the fold

### First-Call Decks / Ads
- Lead with **outcomes** first
- Bring the Hook after pain is acknowledged

### Thought Leadership / Blogs
- Lead with **category vision** (Hook + Anchor)
- Ladder down into proof and outcomes
- Use narrative structure: problem → framework → examples → call to action

### Case Studies
- Blend outcomes + how
- Keep U→U→A as organizing device
- Always include: challenge, solution, quantified results, customer quote

### Executive Comms / PR
- Headline can carry vision
- First paragraph must include quantified proof

## Writing Patterns from Enterpret Content

### Opening Hooks
**Narrative start**:
> "Your first 100 customers knew you cared. You met them in person, remembered their child's birthday..."

**Problem recognition**:
> "Most teams don't realize they have outgrown their feedback tools until something breaks."

**Bold statement**:
> "Speed without intelligence is not progress. It is chaos."

### Framework Naming
Give problems and solutions memorable names:
- "The Scaling Paradox"
- "The Three Phases of Feedback Maturity"
- "Root Cause Elimination"
- "Unify. Understand. Act."

### Contrast Patterns
Use parallel structure to show before/after:
> "Personal emails became automated responses. Overnight fixes became 'planned for next quarter.' First names became account numbers."

### Closing Patterns
**Mirror the opening**:
> "Your early success came from authentic customer connection. Your future success depends on scaling that connection."

**Movement language**:
> "This is more than a launch. It is the start of a movement."

**Clear next step**:
> "If you are spending more time organizing feedback than using it to ship meaningful work, you have crossed the threshold. It is time to upgrade your system."

## Golden Rule

Every message must answer:
1. **Why anything?** Feedback chaos is unsustainable.
2. **Why Enterpret?** We turn signals into owned work and measurable outcomes in your business context.
3. **Why now?** AI has collapsed build cycles; insights must keep pace. Delay = falling behind.
