# Content Templates

Use these templates as starting points. Adapt structure based on specific needs.

---

## Blog Post Template

### Standard Blog Post (800-1500 words)

```markdown
# [Compelling Title with Primary Keyword]

**Hook** (1-2 sentences): Start with a surprising stat, provocative question, or relatable pain point.

**Context** (1 paragraph): Set up the problem or opportunity. Why should the reader care right now?

**Thesis**: State the main insight or argument clearly.

---

## [Section 1: First Key Point]
- Lead with the insight
- Support with example, data, or customer quote
- Connect back to the main thesis

## [Section 2: Second Key Point]
- Same pattern as above
- Use subheads that could stand alone as insights

## [Section 3: Third Key Point]
- Vary your evidence (mix data, anecdotes, expert quotes)

---

## Key Takeaways
- Bullet 1: Actionable insight
- Bullet 2: Actionable insight
- Bullet 3: Actionable insight

## [CTA aligned with content goal]
- Demo request: "See how [specific capability] works..."
- Newsletter: "Get weekly insights on [topic]..."
- Content: "Read our [related piece] for more on..."
```

### Thought Leadership Post (1500-2500 words)

Same structure as above but with:
- Deeper industry context in intro
- 4-5 sections instead of 3
- More original research/data
- Stronger point of view / contrarian angle

## Customer Story Template

**Scaffolding**: Context → Motivation (Use Cases) → Conclusion

Each use case follows: **Tension → Change → Meaning**

```markdown
# How [Customer] [Achieved Transformation] with Enterpret

## At a Glance
- **Company**: [Name, brief description, scale]
- **Industry**: [Sector]
- **Key Outcomes**: [2-3 headline metrics]

---

## Context

[Set the stage: Who is this company? What do they do? Why does customer feedback matter to their business?]

[1-2 paragraphs establishing:]
- Company mission and scale
- Why being customer-centric is critical to their model
- The environment they operate in (growth stage, market, user base)

> "[Quote establishing who they are and what they care about]"
> — [Name], [Title] at [Company]

---

## Motivation

[Why did they need Enterpret? What were they trying to achieve?]

[Brief intro to their goals and the gap between where they were and where they wanted to be]

---

### Use Case 1: [Name the use case, e.g., "Scaling Feedback Analysis"]

**Tension**
[What was broken? What was the pain? Be specific and visceral.]
- What manual process was failing?
- What was the cost (time, money, morale, missed insights)?
- How did this feel for the team?

> "[Quote capturing the frustration or limitation]"

**Change**
[What shifted when they implemented Enterpret for this use case?]
- What do they do differently now?
- What capability unlocked?
- How does the workflow look today vs. before?

**Meaning**
[Why does this change matter? Connect to business outcomes.]
- Specific metric or result
- Impact on team, customers, or business
- What this enables going forward

> "[Quote about the transformation or result]"

---

### Use Case 2: [Name the use case, e.g., "Connecting Feedback to Revenue"]

**Tension**
[Different pain point or limitation]
- What couldn't they do before?
- What was the business risk or missed opportunity?

**Change**
[How Enterpret addressed this]
- New capability or workflow
- Integration or feature that made the difference

**Meaning**
[Outcome and significance]
- Quantified result if available
- Strategic importance

> "[Quote about this specific use case]"

---

### Use Case 3: [Name the use case, e.g., "Proactive Issue Detection"]

**Tension**
[Third dimension of the problem]

**Change**
[Third transformation]

**Meaning**
[Third outcome]

> "[Quote]"

---

## Conclusion / The Bigger Meaning

[Zoom out: What does this all add up to?]

[1-2 paragraphs on:]
- The cumulative impact across use cases
- How their relationship with customers has transformed
- What this means for their future

> "[Capstone quote that captures the full transformation]"
> — [Name], [Title] at [Company]

**Ready to write your own story?** [CTA]
```

### Customer Story Writing Tips

**Tension section**:
- Be specific about the pain (not "it was hard" but "we spent 20 hours/week manually tagging")
- Include emotional language where authentic
- Show the stakes (what was at risk?)

**Change section**:
- Focus on the "how" — what did they actually do differently?
- Name specific Enterpret features/capabilities
- Show the workflow transformation

**Meaning section**:
- Always include a metric when possible
- Connect to business outcomes (revenue, retention, efficiency)
- Show why this matters beyond the immediate result

**Quotes**:
- Place at emotional peaks (end of tension, end of meaning)
- Use verbatim customer language — don't polish too much
- Ensure each quote adds something the prose doesn't
